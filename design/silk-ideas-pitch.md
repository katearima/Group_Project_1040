# Ideas Pitch (11/03/2026)

## MIT Bathroom / Water Fountain rating app! (Top idea)

User submitted ratings for MIT's water fountains and bathrooms so you will always know which ones to go to. Water Fountains can be rated by temperature, taste, pressure, and bathroom can be rated by clealiness, stall size, etc. See what everyone thinks! I actually really like this because this is just a rating app but the flavor/reskin is silly enough that it makes me want to do it, and I would also be very happy if something like this existed for real.

## Emotion Visualizer

An extremely abstract idea, but I've always some kind of pocket app where I could type in some words, whatever words, and it would analyze those words and return me a visual display of how I'm feeling. This could be some color splash, or colored balls dropping onto my screen, or just something. Not really sure how I'd make this a dynamic website though. Maybe you can save whatever visual you produced, and you can also compare it to others, or maybe it could be optionally collaborative.

## D&D Character Visualizer

This is honestly more flavor than bad situation, but I've always wanted to make some sort of interactable, dynamic character sheet for D&D that shows what your character looks like as you make them. Specifically I imagine a humanoid avatar display on screen and you can click on various parts of it to see various stats and features of your character. I understand this requires both a very good grasp of D&D 5e and art, and so is maybe not too applicable for a team project like this.
