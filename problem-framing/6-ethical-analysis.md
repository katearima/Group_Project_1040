### Indirect Stakeholders
Note: for brevity, we omit the indirect stakeholders inherent to all platform-style apps, such as the need for app hosting indirectly increasing demand for data centers.

#### Startups
Startups that do not use our platform will generally have the same goals and values as those who do. If our platform accomplishes its goal and becomes broadly use, startups may feel that they are forced to use it in order to maintain competitiveness.

#### Prospective Employees and Builders
Prospective employees and builders (and other people looking for work) that do not use our platform may suffer in an employer-centric job market. If our platform is highly successful, it may cause some startups to forgo other methods of searching for employees, which could prevent those who do not use the platform from finding those opportunities. In particular, those who value personal connections and more organic networking over the sort of algorithmic matching we will offer may especially suffer as our platform pushes startup culture and hiring further from their ideals.

#### Businesses
**Goals** may include: Hire new employees; retain current employees; maintain business model
**Values**: Growth; Stability; Profit
Because our platform is not intended for businesses, they will likely be unable to take advantage of it in order to find prospective employees to hire. In an employee-centric job market, this could negatively affect their ability to find people to hire. In the worst case, if they find themselves unable to fill a position that they might have been able to fill if not for prospective employees finding work via our platform, we may have directly hampered their stability and growth
